## 🌌 Project Overview

**LAST ORBIT** is a **roguelike** strategy, resource-management, and narrative survival game featuring retro aerospace console _pixel art_, developed for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge**.

You step into the boots of **Luke**, a newly appointed Commander in NASA's Rapid Response Division. At **02:47 UTC**, an emergency transmission from Houston alerts command to the discovery of **3I/Nyx**, the third confirmed interstellar object passing through our Solar System. This is your first operation in command, and **the entire future of your career depends on what happens over the next 72 hours**: achieving a historic scientific breakthrough will cement your legacy at the agency, while returning empty-handed—or failing to make it back—will mean the end of your career.

Designed as a **roguelike** experience, **no two runs are ever the same**. Careful pre-launch organization, smart resource management, and every decision you make directly shape what you uncover and your chances of bringing your crew home alive.

## 🛠️ Mission Assembly (What You Choose)

Success begins before liftoff. Under strict operational constraints, you must organize your expedition by choosing:

- **Your Spacecraft:** Select between three distinct vessel classes (**Pegaso-I**, **Hércules-IV**, or **Talos-IA**), each built around a different engineering philosophy with unique structural advantages, crew capacities, and operational trade-offs.
- **Scientific Payload:** Equip one specialized exploration instrument (**MIS-Lynceus**, **SAL-Phoebus**, or **D2C2-Prometeo**) depending on the analytical method and depth of study you want to attempt on **3I/Nyx**.
- **Specialist Crew:** Assemble your team from four experts (**Danko**, **Dr. Candy**, **Chem. Jan**, and **Eng. Najo**), balancing technical expertise, human resilience, and ship requirements.

## 🔭 Space Concepts Introduced

Through its interactive systems, **LAST ORBIT** introduces players to real concepts in astrophysics and aerospace engineering:

- **Interstellar Objects & Hyperbolic Trajectories:** Inspired by real visitors such as _1I/'Oumuamua_ and _2I/Borisov_, highlighting the difference between periodic comets bound to the Sun in elliptical orbits and interstellar travelers crossing the Solar System on an open, one-way hyperbolic path.
- **Cometary Anatomy & Sublimation:** Showing how solar heating transforms a comet's icy nucleus into a glowing _coma_ (an ionized cloud of gas and dust) rich in water ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$), methane ($\text{CH}_4$), and prebiotic molecules essential to understanding the origins of life.
- **Real Space Exploration Instrumentation:** Presenting three authentic methods of remote and contact planetary science: infrared and mass spectrometry (_ROSINA/NIRSpec_), Laser-Induced Breakdown Spectroscopy (_LIBS_ / _SuperCam_), and microgravity cryogenic drilling (_Philae_'s _SD2_).
